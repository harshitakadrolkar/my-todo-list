# My To-Do List ✦

*Make your study goals easier, one task at a time.*

A simple and aesthetic To-Do List designed for students who want to stay organized without feeling overwhelmed. Whether you're planning your study schedule, keeping track of assignments, preparing for exams, or setting small daily goals, this website helps you manage everything in one place.

Add your tasks, track your progress, and celebrate every little achievement. Because even the smallest steps bring you closer to your goals! 🌱

## Features
- 📚 Organize your study tasks and daily goals.
- ✏️ Easily add, edit, and delete tasks.
- ⭐ Mark completed tasks and track your progress.
- 🌿 Filter tasks to focus on what's left to do.
- 💚 Enjoy a clean, simple interface that keeps you focused.

**Your study journey, one small step at a time.**
